Privacy Policy – PW Bot

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

PW Bot is an internal WhatsApp-based reporting assistant designed to help authorized users request and receive business reports and operational information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users interact with PW Bot.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how the service is used, PW Bot may process:

  • WhatsApp phone numbers and sender identifiers.
  • Messages and commands sent to the bot.
  • Information required to identify and deliver requested reports.
  • Technical logs required for troubleshooting and service operation.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Understand report requests and commands.
  • Generate and deliver requested business reports.
  • Respond to user queries and provide bot functionality.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the service.
  • Protect the service against unauthorized use.

4. Data Sharing

PW Bot does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the bot, including WhatsApp/Meta, hosting providers and other configured technical services.

Information may also be shared with authorized personnel where necessary to provide the requested reporting service or comply with applicable law.

5. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by the service. Information is retained only as necessary for the operational, business and legal purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable retention requirements.
